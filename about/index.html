About.

I'm Arpan Verma, a Masters student in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at the Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology, Delhi, where I also did my B.Tech. My B.Tech thesis, in the Mobility and Optimization Lab with Dr. Pravesh Biyani, uses perturbation analysis and integer programming to make urban bus transit faster.

I enjoy problem solving in most of its forms, whether that's an integer program, a model that won't converge or a line of a poem that won't scan.
