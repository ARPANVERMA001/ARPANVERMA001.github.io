About

I'm a Masters student in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at IIIT Delhi, where I also did my B.Tech. For my B.Tech thesis I joined the Mobility and Optimization Lab with Dr. Pravesh Biyani, on making urban bus transit faster for the people who ride it.

I like problems that sit between a clean mathematical model and a messy real system, and lately, questions about what our evaluations of ML systems actually show. I've built ML pipelines, packing optimizers, a memory allocator and a small arcade game along the way. Away from the terminal, I play the piano, write poems and read about geopolitics.
