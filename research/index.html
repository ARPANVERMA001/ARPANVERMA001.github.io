01 — B.Tech thesis · June 2024 – present

Urban bus transit

In the Mobility and Optimization Lab at IIIT Delhi, advised by Dr. Pravesh Biyani, I study how to make city bus networks faster for the people who ride them. The question is simple to state: given how passengers actually travel, how should routes be planned to bring everyone's arrival time down?

  • Optimizing urban bus transit systems with perturbation analysis and integer programming.
  • Analyzing more than a million passenger trips to understand demand and minimize estimated time of arrival.
  • Developing algorithms that optimize public-transit routes.
  • Related tooling: a real-time bus tracker built with Flask and SQLite on Delhi's open GTFS transit feed.

02 — Walmart Sparkathon

Packing & logistics

LoadLogic treats loading a truck as a three-dimensional packing problem. Formulated as a linear integer program, it decides where each box goes so that trucks leave fuller, and an interactive Three.js view lets a planner inspect the result before anything is loaded. Watch the demo.

03 — Hackathons & coursework

Learning & optimization

I keep returning to the overlap between the two fields: optimization methods that train models, and models that make optimization practical. At the EPOCH Hackathon (Infosys Centre for AI, March 2024) my team of four won with a pipeline-construction model optimized by gradient descent and Adam. In a traffic project I forecast intersection flows with LDA, random forests, XGBoost and LSTMs, then used those forecasts to retime signals, cutting average wait time by 42%.
